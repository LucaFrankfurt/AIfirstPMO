Kolibri is one app, not a website with a smaller sibling. The phone layout is the same code
drawing the same data — it just has less room.

## Install it

**On iOS** — open the instance in Safari, *Share → Add to Home Screen*.
**On Android** — Chrome offers *Install app*, or *Add to Home Screen* in the menu.
**On a desktop** — the install button in the address bar.

Installed, it gets its own icon, opens without browser chrome, and keeps its own storage. That
last part is what makes the offline half real rather than theoretical: a tab you closed is a tab
the browser may evict; an installed app is not.

## What changes at phone width

- The sidebar becomes a **tab bar** at the foot: My work, Inbox, Chat, Search, More.
- The project header's controls collapse into compact forms rather than wrapping onto a second
  row.
- Boards scroll sideways one column at a time.
- Task detail opens as a full sheet rather than a panel.

The layout is checked at every width from 340 to 1600 pixels, in 20-pixel steps, for a page that
scrolls sideways, a one-row bar that has grown a second row, and a title that has been squeezed
away. That is not decoration: the check exists because a layout came apart between 880 and 940
pixels and nobody had happened to open a window that size.

## Why offline matters most here

A phone is the device that is actually on a train. Everything in
[working offline](/beyond/offline/) applies, and two things are worth setting up before you need
them:

**Install it** (above), so the storage sticks.

**Turn on a banner** for the phone specifically — *Settings → Notifications*, on the phone. Push
permission belongs to a browser, so switching it on at your desk does nothing for your pocket.
See [notifications](/people/notifications/).

## Things that are easier on a phone than you expect

- **Pasting a screenshot into a comment or a chat message.** It uploads and downscales in the
  browser.
- **Starting a [timer](/planning/time/)** and letting it run. It survives the app being closed,
  the phone sleeping and a tunnel.
- **Voice dictation into the quick-add box.** The one-line syntax reads fine when spoken —
  *"fix the login redirect bang high at ada"* comes out as a titled, prioritised, assigned task.

## Things that are not

The timeline and the table layout want a wide screen. They work; they are not pleasant. Do
planning at a desk.
