*Project → Reports → The link.* You get a URL. Anybody who opens it sees a form.

No session, no account, no script — it works on any phone, including one on a building site with
one bar of signal. That is who it is for: the person reporting the thing, who does not work here
and should not have to be onboarded to tell you about it.

## What happens to what arrives

It goes into the project's **Reports** tab, not onto the board — under **Waiting** until somebody
deals with it, and **Dealt with** afterwards.

Somebody on the team opens that tab, reads the submission, and either accepts it — which creates a
real task — or declines it. A declined report stays in the list, marked, so nobody triages it
twice. **Nothing from outside becomes a task on its own.**

That is the whole point of the feature. A public form that files directly into the backlog is a
public form that fills the backlog with duplicates, tests and abuse, and a team that stops
looking at the backlog.

## Accepting one

Accepting opens the ordinary new-task sheet, prefilled from the submission. Two things worth
noticing:

- **The title is yours, not theirs.** You can rewrite it, and you usually should — what somebody
  outside calls a problem and what the team calls the work are rarely the same sentence. What they
  wrote becomes the task's description, with whatever they left of their name.
- **Everything else is a normal task from that moment**: assign it, label it, put it in a cycle.

A submitter's name is shown as *not verified*, because it is a string somebody typed into a public
form and nothing checked it.

## What the reporter sees

The form, a confirmation, and nothing else. They do not get an account, cannot see the Reports tab,
cannot see the project, and are not told what happened to their report — because they have no
identity here to tell. If a conversation is needed, that is an email or a
[shared page with a note](/pages/sharing/#letting-them-write-back), not this.

## Turning it off

Revoking the link stops it working. Anything already under **Waiting** stays there.

:::caution[Treat the link as public]
Anybody who has it can submit. It is rate limited by address, but a link posted somewhere public
will attract what public links attract — so put it where the people you want it from are, and
rotate it if it ends up somewhere else.
:::
